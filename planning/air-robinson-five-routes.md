# AIR ROBINSON — 5 proposed world-flight routes

Status: concept / preview only. Not a finalized itinerary. Do not publish to production without owner approval.

1. Pacific route: Japan → Hawaii → western North America → South Pacific → Japan.
2. Eurasian heritage route: Japan → East Asia → Central Asia → Europe → Japan.
3. Indian Ocean route: Japan → Southeast Asia → Indian Ocean → East Africa → Japan.
4. Atlantic route: Japan → Europe → Atlantic islands → eastern Americas → Japan.
5. Southern hemisphere route: Japan → Oceania → southern South America → southern Africa → Japan.

Landing locations and exact heritage sites: provisional; require geographic and presentation review.

Preview requirements: 21:9 desktop viewport, responsive iPhone viewport, scenic flight background, 140% globe scale option for long ocean/land passages.
